Community: r/TheBeatles

These labels describe how a post expresses an opinion, not whether
the opinion is correct or whether I agree with it.

explained_opinion:
The writer expresses an opinion about the Beatles or their music
and supports it with a specific feature, example, or personal
experience that explains the opinion.

stated_opinion:
The writer expresses an opinion about the Beatles or their music
but does not give a specific supporting explanation.

other:
The writer does not express their own opinion, such as a post
that only asks a question or shares factual information.

Decision rules:
1. Read the full title and body together.
2. If there is no opinion from the writer, choose other.
3. If there is an opinion with a specific supporting explanation,
   choose explained_opinion.
4. If there is an opinion without that support, choose stated_opinion.
5. General praise or criticism, such as "amazing," "beautiful,"
   "the best," or "terrible," is not a supporting explanation.
6. Mentioning vocals, instruments, or lyrics is not enough by itself.
   The writer must explain what about them supports the opinion.
7. A fact must help explain the opinion. An unrelated date or
   song title does not make an opinion explained.
8. Starting with a question does not automatically make a post other.
   Check whether the writer also expresses and supports an opinion.
9. If a post contains several opinions, choose explained_opinion
   when at least one has a specific supporting explanation.
10. Opinions include both praise and criticism. Describing a song
    as too long or saying it loses the writer's interest counts
    as a judgment.
11. Questions such as "Who else thinks..." can express the
    writer's own opinion. A question mark does not determine the label.
12. A factual statement or question alongside an unsupported
    opinion does not change it to other.

Hardest boundary:
explained_opinion versus stated_opinion.

Boundary rule:
Choose explained_opinion only when a specific detail explains
why the writer holds an opinion. The word "because" alone does
not count as an explanation.
